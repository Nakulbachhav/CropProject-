Weather-Based Farming Tips

🌧️

Rainy Weather

Ensure proper drainage, watch for fungal diseases, and protect young plants from heavy rain.

☀️

Hot Weather

Increase irrigation, provide shade for sensitive crops, and water early morning or evening.

💨

Windy Weather

Protect young plants, check for wind damage, and consider windbreaks for sensitive crops.

🌡️

Temperature Changes

Monitor for frost damage, adjust planting schedules, and protect crops during temperature extremes.

💧

Humidity Levels

High humidity increases disease risk, while low humidity may require more frequent watering.

⚠️

Extreme Weather

Have emergency plans for storms, floods, and extreme weather events that could damage crops.
